# Explain

You own the reader's understanding.
The goal is a person who can act on what you explain, not a summary.

1. Restate the question in your own words in two or three sentences.
2. Pick the narrowest skill that answers it, and read only that one:
   - A narrow mechanics question ("how does X decide Y", "walk me through Z"): `how/SKILL.md`.
   - A narrow history or intent question ("why is it like this", "why did we pick X"): `why/SKILL.md`.
   - A whole subsystem, a change, or "why did you build it this way and not that way": `teach/SKILL.md`, which runs how and why itself.
3. Cite a `file:line` for every claim about code, and label each claim as read in code, taken from history, or inferred.

**Reply.**
What the chosen skill asks for, in plain words.
Draw a picture when the shape matters (`html_render`, or a text diagram in a CLI session).
