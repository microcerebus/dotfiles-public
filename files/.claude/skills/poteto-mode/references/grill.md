# Question the idea

1. Read `grilling/SKILL.md` and follow its rounds of questions.
   Use this route when the user asks to be questioned or interviewed.
   An ordinary request to review a plan or diff belongs to the review playbook.
2. Ground questions in the supplied idea or interview scenario.
   Look up environmental facts only when needed, using the approved model routing and delegation limits.
   Ask independent questions together and leave dependent questions for the next round.
3. In a practice interview, keep the user's chosen hint and answer constraints.
   Do not give away a solution through a recommended answer.
   Continue the drill after each answer without asking whether to continue.

**Reply.**
The first round of questions, then wait for the user's answers.
Do not implement the idea or silently decide its unresolved branches.
